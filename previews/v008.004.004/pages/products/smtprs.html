Output

Built for product and operations teams.

smtpRS returns a small set of fields that can support onboarding, review queues, internal tooling, and trust automation.

risk_score Normalized risk posture for the submitted email identity
verdict A recommended decision path for the workflow
reasons Short explanations that support review and routing
confidence A model confidence band for downstream action

Specialized models, production shape.

smtpRS is designed for narrow, high-frequency trust decisions where a general-purpose model is too broad and a rules-only system is too brittle.
