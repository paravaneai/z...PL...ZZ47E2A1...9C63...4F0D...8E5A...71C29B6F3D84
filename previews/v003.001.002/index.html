Capabilities

AI and ML work across several model families.

Paravane focuses on practical systems that can be tested, monitored, and integrated into real workflows instead of isolated demos.

01

Large language models

LLM-assisted classification, summarization, structured extraction, prompt workflows, tool use, and reasoning support.

02

Sequence models

LSTM and sequence-oriented methods for pattern recognition, local context, anomaly signals, and behavior over ordered inputs.

03

Signal scoring

Compact models and rules that convert raw indicators into scores, confidence bands, reason codes, and review-ready outputs.

Approach

Model work should be explainable, measurable, and useful.

We combine modern language models with smaller specialized models, deterministic rules, and evaluation loops. The goal is not to use AI for its own sake; it is to produce outputs that a system, operator, or product can actually act on.
