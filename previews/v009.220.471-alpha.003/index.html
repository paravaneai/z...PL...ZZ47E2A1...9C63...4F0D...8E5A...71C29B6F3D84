The Paravane approach

From raw signals to a defensible next action.

We do not start with broad automation. We start with the decision, then build the smallest useful intelligence system around it.

01

Connect

Bring in the records, events, and evidence already produced by your systems.

02

Evaluate

Normalize the inputs and calculate the signals that matter to the decision.

03

Explain

Return a score, confidence, reason codes, and the evidence behind the result.

04

Route

Send the result into the workflow, queue, or application that owns the action.

Built to fit your stack

Start narrow. Integrate quickly. Keep control.

Paravane products are designed to sit beside your current systems, not replace them. Use an API, import, or connector to bring signals in, then route structured outputs back to the people and software that own the decision.

  • ✓ Clear contracts and structured outputs
  • ✓ Confidence and evidence kept beside the decision
  • ✓ Human review and policy remain inside your workflow
