What NeuralScore does

Email intelligence for modern trust systems.

NeuralScore is a family of AI models from Paravane Labs that evaluates email identity signals and returns compact, decision-ready risk intelligence for product and operations teams.

Paravane Labs develops specialized AI systems for decisions that need speed, calibration, and traceability. NeuralScore brings that work to email risk: a narrow model family built for the places where trust decisions quietly shape product outcomes.

Model family

Two profiles for different decision environments.

NeuralScore Mini and NeuralScore Large are tuned for different operating conditions, from high-volume screening to more sensitive review paths.
