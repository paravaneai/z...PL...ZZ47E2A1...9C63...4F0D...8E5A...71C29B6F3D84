Before risk reaches the forecast

Know which accounts need attention and why.

RetainIQ sits beside CRM, customer-success, support, billing, and product systems as a decision-support layer. It prioritizes accounts without forcing teams to replace the tools and workflows they already use.

HealthyKeep the normal customer-success motion moving. WatchInspect a weakening signal or confidence gap. At riskAssign an owner and a practical retention action.

Account intelligence loop

From scattered customer signals to an explainable next action.

RetainIQ resolves incoming signals into an account timeline, calculates health and supporting risks, explains what changed, and recommends the next customer-success action.

01NormalizeResolve CRM, usage, support, billing, and engagement events to the right account.
02ScoreCalculate account health, supporting risks, trend, confidence, and revenue at risk.
03ExplainRank the signal changes and evidence that materially moved the account.
04ActRecommend, assign, and track a practical retention action with human ownership.

Focused signals

Start with the account changes customer teams can act on.

The first RetainIQ release is deliberately narrow. It emphasizes explainable account signals, visible confidence, and manageable action queues instead of pretending to be a CRM replacement.

01

Usage and adoption decline

Track active users, seat utilization, feature depth, milestone progress, and material changes against the account's own baseline.

02

Support pain and escalation

Surface high-severity tickets, reopened issues, aging cases, escalation patterns, and negative support sentiment.

03

Stakeholder disengagement

Identify sponsor silence, missed touchpoints, declining response activity, and uncertain champion coverage.

04

Renewal and billing pressure

Combine renewal proximity, contract value, payment friction, downgrade signals, and prior renewal behavior.

Revenue-level prioritization

Move from account risk to a book-of-business decision.

RetainIQ connects account health to ARR or MRR, renewal timing, ownership, top risk drivers, action status, and outcome feedback so leaders can see where attention protects revenue.

Confidence before certainty

Show what the score knows, what changed, and what data is missing.

RetainIQ is designed to remain useful with partial data without hiding uncertainty. Missing, stale, or weakly matched sources reduce confidence and stay visible beside the score and recommendation.

01

Collect the account signals available from connected systems or practical CSV imports.

02

Resolve source records to the right account and flag uncertain mappings for review.

03

Explain score movement through source-linked drivers, time windows, and freshness.

04

Learn from saved, renewed, churned, ignored, and false-positive outcomes.

In development

Prioritization first. Explanations always.

RetainIQ is being designed to recommend practical customer-success actions while account owners retain final judgment. It will not silently automate outreach or present weak data as certain prediction.
