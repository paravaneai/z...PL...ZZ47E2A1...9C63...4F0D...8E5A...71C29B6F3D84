What smtpRS does

Email intelligence for modern trust systems.

smtpRS is a family of AI models from Paravane Labs that evaluates email identity signals and returns compact, decision-ready risk intelligence for product and operations teams.

Paravane Labs develops specialized AI systems for decisions that need speed, calibration, and traceability. smtpRS brings that work to email risk: a narrow model family built for the places where trust decisions quietly shape product outcomes.

Model family

smtpRS Mini and smtpRS Large are tuned for different operating conditions, from high-volume screening to more sensitive review paths.
