Signal flow

From raw email to action.

EmailRisk is designed to return enough context for automation and enough explanation for a human reviewer.

Step 01

Normalize

Clean the address, split local/domain parts, and prepare consistent inputs.

Step 02

Inspect

Check domain, mailbox, MX, disposable, velocity, and reputation-style patterns.

Step 03

Score

Convert signals into a severity level, score, and reason code set.

Step 04

Act

Return a recommended action such as allow, monitor, challenge, review, or block.

API shape

Clean responses your app can use immediately.

Use the score for automation. Use the reason codes for logging, dashboards, and review queues. Keep the result explainable from day one.
