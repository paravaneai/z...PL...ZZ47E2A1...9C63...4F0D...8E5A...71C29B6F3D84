smtpRS Email Validation & Risk Scoring API

smtpRS is Paravane's real-time email validation and risk scoring API. It validates email addresses and evaluates public sender, domain, mail, and authentication signals, then returns a normalized risk score, an allow, review, or reject recommendation, and supporting evidence in one documented API response.
