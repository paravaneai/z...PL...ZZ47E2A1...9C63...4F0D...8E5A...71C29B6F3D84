Evaluation

More than a deliverability check.

EmailRisk combines validation, reputation, identity, and pattern signals into a small review-ready response.

Mailbox

Address quality

Format, mailbox shape, route posture, and normalized traits.

Domain

Domain context

DNS posture, provider behavior, domain patterns, and reputation depth.

Model

Risk inference

Weighted signals for suspicious combinations and low-trust patterns.

Ops

Decision shape

Compact fields that can be routed into review or automation logic.

Output

Readable fields for operational review.

The response is intentionally small: enough to support a decision without requiring a separate analyst view.

risk_score 0-100 normalized risk score
verdict allow · review · throttle · block
reasons Compact reason codes for review queues
confidence Model certainty band
traits Normalized mailbox and domain attributes

Workflow fit

Built to sit behind existing systems.

EmailRisk can support intake forms, onboarding flows, lead quality review, abuse queues, fraud triage, and internal review tools.
