Our story

The company came after the software.

The technology behind smtpRS began in 2022 as MailGuard, software built for private clients and deployed into active environments from the start.

For nearly four years, the platform remained private while it was operated, refined, and expanded around real client use. That meant learning from production conditions before asking the broader market to use it.

Paravane Labs was established in 2026 to give that work a dedicated software company. On August 19, 2026, smtpRS became our first publicly available product.

How we operate

A product company shaped by operating reality.

We build focused software around recurring operational decisions—where a useful signal can help a person or team see something sooner, investigate with better context, or act with more confidence.

01

Products, not generic platforms.

Each Paravane product is centered on a defined operational problem, a clear set of inputs, and outputs that are meant to be understood and used—not simply generated.

Email intelligenceInvoice protectionRetention intelligenceVendor intelligence
02

Production first.

Our first product spent years in real client environments before public launch. That operating history still informs how we think about reliability and usefulness.

03

Signals over noise.

We focus on surfacing the small number of changes, anomalies, and risk indicators that deserve attention—not adding another stream of undifferentiated data.

04

Human-readable by design.

Useful intelligence should help people evaluate what happened and why. We design around structured outputs, supporting context, and clear boundaries.
