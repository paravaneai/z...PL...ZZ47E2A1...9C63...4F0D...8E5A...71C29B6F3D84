Operating workflow

From fragmented records to a defensible vendor review.

VendorLens resolves duplicate and changing records into a canonical vendor profile, calculates trust and dependency scores, explains what changed, and creates a review plan with source-linked evidence.

01

Resolve

Map aliases, subsidiaries, domains, contacts, and payment references to the correct vendor.

02

Score

Calculate reliability, compliance, dependency, financial exposure, change risk, and confidence.

03

Explain

Show reason codes, score deltas, evidence freshness, and the business areas affected.

04

Review

Assign the right owner, due date, remediation step, and auditable outcome.

Governance standard

Useful intelligence without hidden certainty.

VendorLens is designed for decisions that need visible evidence, understandable limits, and accountable human ownership.

01

Importance is not wrongdoing

A critical vendor is not automatically a bad vendor; dependency and trust remain separate.

02

Evidence stays legible

03

Governance stays human
