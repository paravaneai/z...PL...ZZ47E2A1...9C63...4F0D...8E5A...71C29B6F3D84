1. Overview

This Cookie Notice explains how Paravane Labs, LLC ("Paravane," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies, local storage, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies on our websites, dashboards, documentation, checkout flows, and related Services.

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device. Similar technologies may store identifiers, preferences, or event data locally or transmit usage information to us or our service providers.

4. Your choices

Where required, Paravane will provide a cookie banner, preference center, or similar control for optional cookies. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent the website, dashboard, checkout, or security features from working correctly.

You can opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in the email. Cookie choices do not necessarily unsubscribe you from email communications, and email choices do not necessarily change cookies stored on your device.

5. Global Privacy Control and other opt-out signals

Current recommended position: Paravane does not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and advertising cookies should remain disabled unless Paravane implements proper notice, consent, and opt-out controls. If Paravane later engages in covered sale/share or targeted-advertising processing, it should honor recognized opt-out preference signals, such as Global Privacy Control, where required by applicable law.

6. Third-party cookies and embedded services

Third-party services may set their own cookies or similar technologies when you interact with embedded checkout, support, documentation, video, analytics, or security features. Their use of cookies is governed by their own policies, except where they process information on Paravane's behalf as service providers.

7. Changes to this Cookie Notice

We may update this Cookie Notice as our websites, dashboard, providers, or technologies change. The updated version will be indicated by a new last-updated date.
