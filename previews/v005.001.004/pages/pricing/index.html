Credits

Different scoring modes consume different credits.

Credit pricing keeps simple checks inexpensive while protecting deeper, slower, or more sensitive infrastructure paths.

Mode Credit cost Notes
Mini score 1 credit Fast screening and public demo profile.
Full score 3 credits Broader analysis for paid tiers.
Strict/deep score 5 credits Deeper passive checks for Pro and above.
Catch-all SMTP check 20 credits Manual approval only because it is slower and more sensitive.
Bulk upload Per row A 1,000-row Mini upload uses 1,000 credits.
