Signals

More than valid or invalid.

Email risk is rarely a single check. Paravane returns a compact risk view built from multiple layers.

S

Syntax and format

Normalize the obvious issues before the model layer spends time on deeper signals.

D

Domain posture

Read DNS, MX, recency, disposable infrastructure, and domain stability features.

M

Mailbox confidence

Surface deliverability and mailbox uncertainty without making the response noisy.

R

Reputation patterns

Flag throwaway, abusive, role-based, or suspicious behavior patterns.

A

AI scoring

Compress low-level indicators into a risk score, confidence score, and reason codes.

Q

Queue-friendly output

Return enough context for allow, block, review, suppression, or enrichment workflows.

Response

A clean answer for product flows.

Use the simple fields for automation. Use the reason codes when a human needs to understand why the model leaned a certain way.

Fields

What teams can use.

Field Purpose Typical use
verdict Final model-assisted recommendation. Allow, review, block, suppress.
risk_score Continuous risk score for threshold tuning. Automation and routing.
confidence How strongly the signal stack supports the answer. Escalation and QA.
checks Readable validation and reputation attributes. Debugging and reporting.
signals Reason codes behind the verdict. Review queues and audit notes.

Is this just email validation?

No. Validation is one layer. Paravane is positioned as an email intelligence and risk signal system.

Does it expose the model?

No. The API returns useful verdicts and signals without exposing internal weights or detection logic.

Where does it fit?

Signup forms, lead intake, enrichment workflows, fraud review, and internal risk tooling.

What stage is it in?

Controlled preview. The page is ready for public positioning while access remains limited.
